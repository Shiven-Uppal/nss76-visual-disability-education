# Manuscript insertions for Supplementary Table S1

## Methods insertion

Place after the description of the main support-associated analysis:

> **Age-restricted sensitivity analyses.** To assess whether the principal association was driven by older cohorts with potentially different historical service access and recall conditions, the direct-standardisation analysis was repeated among persons aged 3–17 years and 6–17 years. In each restricted population, eligibility, weighted household-consumption tertiles, common support, and the pooled common-support target distribution were recalculated within the relevant age range. The 3–17-year analysis standardised over age bands 3–10 and 11–17 years; the 6–17-year analysis standardised over age bands 6–10 and 11–17 years. Inference used 200 PSU-within-stratum bootstrap resamples, as in the main association analysis. Results are reported in Supplementary Table S1.

## Results insertion

Place after the main adjusted association result:

> The association was also present in both age-restricted analyses. Among persons aged 3–17 years, the standardised probability of ever ordinary-school enrolment was 53.45% without reported intervention and 90.52% with reported intervention, an adjusted difference of 37.07 percentage points (95% PSU-bootstrap interval: 32.75–42.26). Among persons aged 6–17 years, the corresponding probabilities were 56.26% and 90.60%, an adjusted difference of 34.34 percentage points (95% PSU-bootstrap interval: 29.77–39.86). These sensitivity analyses do not alter the non-causal interpretation: the estimates remain adjusted associations and may still reflect unmeasured differences in access, need, targeting, or household circumstances.
